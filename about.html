About the platform

Meet TubePilot — Your AI-Powered YouTube Creator OS

TubePilot · Create · Optimize · Schedule · Grow

TubePilot is an AI-powered YouTube Creator OS designed to bring important parts of the YouTube creator workflow together in one connected platform.

From discovering content ideas and preparing video metadata to optimizing content, uploading videos, scheduling publishing and reviewing channel performance, TubePilot aims to help creators manage more of their YouTube workflow from one place.

What is TubePilot?

TubePilot is built for YouTube creators who want a more organized way to plan, prepare, optimize and manage their video publishing process. It combines AI-assisted creator tools with video management and channel-related workflows.

What TubePilot helps creators do

  • Discover content ideas: Generate video and content ideas to support the planning process.
  • Prepare video titles: Generate AI-assisted title suggestions for YouTube videos.
  • Create descriptions: Prepare video descriptions with AI assistance.
  • Optimize metadata: Work with YouTube hashtags, tags and metadata suggestions.
  • Improve YouTube SEO: Use SEO assistance to prepare and optimize video content.
  • Work with thumbnails and visuals: Use thumbnail prompts and supported visual analysis workflows.
  • Upload and schedule videos: Manage supported video upload and YouTube scheduling workflows.
  • Understand channel performance: Review available video analytics, channel insights and audit results.
  • Explore competitor insights: Use supported competitor research capabilities.
  • Connect AI-agent workflows: Use TubePilot's supported MCP capabilities with compatible AI-agent workflows.

Who is TubePilot for?

TubePilot is designed for YouTube creators, independent video publishers and creator teams who want to organize their content planning, video optimization and publishing workflows.

Whether a creator is planning a new video, preparing its metadata, scheduling an upload or reviewing available performance insights, TubePilot aims to make these activities easier to manage.

Our product principle

CREATE · OPTIMIZE · SCHEDULE · GROW

Our product principle is to reduce fragmented creator workflows and bring useful YouTube publishing tools into one connected workspace.
