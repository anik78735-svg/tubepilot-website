Data Control & Privacy

Account & Data Deletion Request

At TubePilot, you have complete control over your personal data and connected accounts. You can delete your account at any time either directly from the app or by submitting a web request below.

Option 1

Delete Directly Inside the TubePilot App

If you currently have the TubePilot application installed on your Android device, you can delete your account and clear all stored data instantly:

  1. Open the TubePilot app on your smartphone.
  2. Tap on the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select Settings from the menu.
  4. Scroll down and tap on Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your choice. Your session will be terminated immediately, and all local and remote data deletion will begin.
Option 2

Request Deletion via Web / Email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request manual deletion by sending an email to our official developer support.

Email Instructions:

Send an email to anikkesharwani37@gmail.com with the following details:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request — [Your Registered Email]
  • Body: Please delete my TubePilot account and all associated data linked to my Google Account ([Your Google Email]).

Data Retention & Transparency

What happens when you delete your account?

Data that is permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (Name, Email ID, Profile Picture).
  • All stored OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens for YouTube and Google Drive.
  • Your video upload history, scheduled queues, draft logs, and diamond balance.
  • Analytics and user preference settings.

Data retention period:

Upon submitting a deletion request, your account is immediately disabled. All personal data and security tokens are permanently purged from our primary server databases within 30 days.

Videos uploaded to YouTube:

Videos that have already been uploaded to your YouTube channel will remain on your channel. TubePilot does not delete videos directly from your YouTube account upon account deletion. You can manage or delete your uploaded videos directly inside YouTube Studio.
