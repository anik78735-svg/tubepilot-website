TubePilot Documentation — Getting Started Guide

TubePilot · Create · Optimize · Schedule · Grow

Welcome to the TubePilot documentation. This page provides an overview of the platform and basic guidance for getting started with its YouTube creator tools.

TubePilot is an AI-powered YouTube Creator OS that brings content ideation, video optimization, upload, scheduling and analytics workflows together.

1. Getting Started with TubePilot

  1. Create a TubePilot account using the sign-in options displayed in the app.
  2. Complete the account setup steps shown in the application.
  3. Connect a YouTube channel that you own or are authorized to manage, using the app's Google authorization flow.
  4. Explore available AI ideas, titles, descriptions, hashtags and SEO tools.
  5. Prepare your video and use the upload and scheduling features where available.
  6. Review available video performance and channel insights through the analytics features.

2. Connecting Your YouTube Channel

TubePilot uses the Google authorization flow to connect eligible YouTube accounts.

Connect only a channel that you own or have permission to manage. Review the permissions requested during authorization before continuing.

The features available after connecting a channel depend on account authorization, granted permissions, YouTube API access and applicable platform requirements.

3. AI Content Creation Tools

TubePilot provides AI-assisted tools to support different parts of YouTube content preparation.

  • AI Ideas: Explore video and content ideas.
  • AI Titles: Generate title suggestions for YouTube videos.
  • AI Descriptions: Prepare video descriptions with AI assistance.
  • AI Hashtags: Generate hashtag suggestions for video content.
  • SEO Tools: Prepare YouTube SEO, tags and metadata.
  • Visual Tools: Use supported thumbnail prompts and visual analysis.

4. Video Upload and Scheduling

TubePilot includes supported workflows for uploading videos and managing YouTube publishing schedules.

  1. Prepare the video and its required metadata.
  2. Open the relevant upload workflow in the app.
  3. Follow the upload steps displayed by the application.
  4. Choose a publishing schedule where the feature is available.
  5. Review upcoming scheduled videos in the app.

Successful uploads and publishing depend on account authorization, available permissions, YouTube platform requirements and the current app implementation.

5. Analytics and Channel Insights

TubePilot provides analytics-related features to help users review available video performance and channel information.

  • Review available video analytics.
  • Explore channel insights.
  • Review Channel Audit information.
  • Use supported competitor research features.

Analytics availability and displayed information depend on the connected channel, permissions and available data.

6. Diamonds and Usage

TubePilot uses a Diamond or credit balance for eligible paid usage.

  • Your current balance is displayed in the live app.
  • Package options are shown in the Diamond Store.
  • Feature entitlements depend on the applicable package.
  • Check the latest package details before making a purchase.

Always refer to the live app for current pricing, package details, usage requirements and applicable purchase conditions.

7. YouTube Permissions and Account Security

Connecting a YouTube channel requires the relevant Google authorization and permissions.

The exact functionality available depends on granted scopes, YouTube API access and platform requirements.

Keep your account credentials and authorization tokens private. Do not share passwords or private access tokens with other people.

8. TubePilot MCP

TubePilot has its own Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration layer intended for compatible AI assistants and agent workflows.

Actual operations depend on the tools implemented in the production MCP server, client compatibility, authentication and permissions.

Read the TubePilot MCP documentation for more information.
