TubePilot Features — AI Tools for YouTube Creators

TubePilot · Create · Optimize · Schedule · Grow

TubePilot is an AI-powered YouTube Creator OS designed to bring content planning, video optimization, publishing workflows and channel insights together.

Its features are organized around four key stages of the YouTube creator workflow: Create, Optimize, Schedule & Publish, and Grow.

Create — AI Content Planning and Creation

Plan your YouTube content and prepare supporting creative material with AI-assisted tools.

  • AI Ideas: Generate video and content ideas for your YouTube channel.
  • Description Ideas: Explore ideas and approaches for video descriptions.
  • AI Description Generator: Create AI-assisted descriptions for YouTube videos.
  • Thumbnail Prompts: Work with prompts and supporting thumbnail creation workflows.
  • Visual Workflow: Use supported visual tools to assist with video and thumbnail preparation.

Optimize — YouTube SEO and Metadata

Prepare and improve important video metadata with AI-assisted optimization tools.

  • AI Title Generator: Generate title suggestions for YouTube videos.
  • AI Hashtags: Generate relevant hashtag suggestions for video content.
  • YouTube SEO Optimizer: Get assistance with YouTube video optimization.
  • Tags and Metadata: Prepare tags and other video metadata.
  • Channel Audit: Review available channel audit information.
  • Visual Analyzer: Use supported visual analysis capabilities.

Schedule & Publish — Video Management

Manage supported video upload and publishing workflows for YouTube.

  • Video Upload: Upload videos through supported TubePilot workflows.
  • Smart Scheduling: Schedule videos for planned publishing.
  • Upcoming Schedule: Review and manage upcoming scheduled videos.
  • YouTube Publishing: Use supported publishing workflows subject to account authorization and YouTube platform requirements.

Grow — Analytics and Channel Insights

Review available performance information and channel-related insights to better understand your YouTube activity.

  • Video Analytics: Review available video performance information.
  • Channel Insights: Understand available channel-level information.
  • Competitor Radar: Explore supported competitor research and performance analysis.

AI & Agent Integration — TubePilot MCP

TubePilot has its own Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration layer intended to provide a structured connection path for compatible AI assistants and agent workflows.

Actual available operations depend on the tools implemented in the production MCP server, client compatibility, authentication and permissions.

Learn more on the TubePilot MCP page.

One Connected YouTube Creator Workflow

TubePilot brings together tools that support different parts of a creator's process, from planning video ideas to preparing metadata, managing uploads and reviewing performance.

Its product principle is: CREATE · OPTIMIZE · SCHEDULE · GROW.
