About TubePilot

What TubePilot is

TubePilot is a mobile application, developed and operated by Bharat Cloud Technologies, that helps YouTube content creators upload their videos more easily. TubePilot is built for individual creators and small teams who want to publish consistently without manually uploading every video from a desktop computer.

How TubePilot works

A creator signs in with their own Google Account and connects their own YouTube channel. Once connected, TubePilot lets them:

  • Upload a single video, or several videos in bulk, directly to their YouTube channel from their phone.
  • Optionally connect a folder in their own Google Drive, so TubePilot can automatically pick up new videos from that folder and upload them to their YouTube channel on a daily schedule the creator sets.
  • Set the privacy (public, unlisted, or private) and scheduled publish time for each upload.

Why TubePilot requests Google account access

TubePilot only accesses the creator's own YouTube channel (to upload and manage their videos) and, only if the creator chooses to enable the auto-upload feature, their own selected Google Drive folder (to read video files the creator has placed there). TubePilot does not access any other Google account data, and does not access other users' channels, videos, or Drive files.
