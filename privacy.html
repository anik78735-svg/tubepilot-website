1. Overview

TubePilot ("TubePilot", "we", "us") is developed and operated by Bharat Cloud Technologies. TubePilot provides YouTube-focused creator tools including AI-assisted content preparation, video upload, scheduling and analytics. Google Drive auto-upload and cross-platform social sharing are not part of the current public product scope. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

2. Data we collect

We collect the following categories of data when you use TubePilot:

  • Account information — name, username, email address or phone number, and a password (stored encrypted) or Google sign-in identifier.
  • Video content and metadata — the videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and tags you choose to upload.
  • Usage and billing data — your free-upload balance, diamond wallet balance, and records of diamond purchase requests (including a payment screenshot and UPI reference you submit for manual verification).
  • Device and notification data — push-notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging and OneSignal) so we can alert you about upload status and account activity.
  • Referral data — your referral code and the referral code you applied, if any.

3. Google and YouTube data

TubePilot uses YouTube API Services for supported YouTube connection and publishing workflows. When you connect your YouTube channel, the app requests the Google authorization scopes shown on the consent screen:

  • YouTube upload & management access — used solely to upload videos to your own connected channel, on your instruction or on the schedule you set, and to display your channel name, thumbnail, and subscriber count in the app.
YouTube API Services notice. TubePilot uses YouTube API Services. By using TubePilot, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. For information on how Google collects and processes data, see the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke TubePilot's access to your Google account at any time from the Google Security Settings page, or from within TubePilot's app settings.

4. How we use your data

  • To upload videos to your connected YouTube channel, as you instruct or schedule.
  • To operate the free-upload and diamond credit system, including sending you a notification if your balance runs out.
  • To send you push notifications about upload status, payment approvals, and account activity.
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the service.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use data obtained through Google APIs for advertising purposes.

5. Third-party services we use

TubePilot relies on the following service providers to operate. Each processes only the data necessary for its function:

  • Google / YouTube API Services — to connect and operate supported YouTube features.
  • Cloud storage providers — to temporarily hold your video file during the upload process.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging and OneSignal — to deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Database and hosting infrastructure — to store your account and app data securely.

6. Data retention

Video files are held in temporary storage only for as long as needed to complete the upload to YouTube, and are automatically deleted from our temporary storage within 24 hours of a successful upload. Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain records (for example, payment records) by law.

7. Your rights & choices

  • Disconnect Google access — disconnect your YouTube channel from the app where the control is available, or revoke access directly from your Google Account.
  • Access & correction — you can view and update your profile information within the app.
  • Deletion — you can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see Section 11).
  • Notifications — you can disable push notifications from your device's system settings at any time.

8. Children's privacy

TubePilot is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted connections (TLS/SSL), encrypted password storage, and access controls — to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Effective date" at the top of this page. Continued use of TubePilot after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
